## Hardware note

The CPU-only set is the basis for the paper's "approximately 30 minutes
without requiring a discrete GPU" claim. On a 2024 Apple M-series laptop
the full CPU set completes in roughly that wall-clock. On slower
hardware or a cold HuggingFace cache, the first run of E1 alone may
take an extra 2-3 minutes to download the corpus (~150 MB). The cache
(`experiments/_shared/cache/`) is reused on every subsequent run.

The `S2_spotlight_calibration_replication` script is the slowest CPU
experiment in the set (it sweeps 6 calibration fractions x 10 seeds on
SpotLight's 452-channel layout); the sandboxed-container number of 371 s
is approximately 2-3x what a 2024 M-series laptop measures.
